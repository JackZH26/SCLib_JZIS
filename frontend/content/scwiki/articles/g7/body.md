## Scope

A quantum spin liquid is a proposed quantum many-body state, not simply a material with no detected magnetic order. Real candidate systems require tests that distinguish persistent quantum behavior from glassiness, valence-bond order, disorder and a measurement window that is not yet cold or slow enough. A claim about fractionalization is stronger than an observation of missing magnetic Bragg peaks. [R2](#ref-source-9d15cd9236072f5c) [R4](#ref-source-5e0dcd80e8b64a1b)

RVB, or resonating valence bond, provides a family of theoretical constructions using superpositions of spin-singlet configurations. Anderson's cuprate proposal connected this language to doped correlated insulators. It remains a mechanism proposal rather than a proof that an undoped cuprate parent is an experimentally established spin liquid. In particular, the antiferromagnetic parent-state evidence cannot be replaced by a preferred wavefunction picture. [R1](#ref-source-5a6026d6455bf7a0)

## Mathematical assumptions

A schematic singlet-covering state can be written

$
|\Psi_{\mathrm{RVB}}\rangle
=\sum_C A_C\prod_{(i,j)\in C}
\frac{|\uparrow_i\downarrow_j\rangle-|\downarrow_i\uparrow_j\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}.
$

The amplitudes $A_C$, allowed coverings and constraints define the actual variational state. This expression alone does not establish its normalization, topological order or suitability for a particular material. Different parton and gauge constructions can yield different phases; a generic U(1) state should not automatically be called a Dirac state. [R1](#ref-source-5a6026d6455bf7a0)

Likewise, singlet correlations do not demonstrate superconductivity. A charged coherent condensate, an insulating spin system and a variational pairing amplitude are distinct physical objects. BCS mean-field language can organize pairing beyond a phonon-only interaction, so a simple “BCS versus RVB” dichotomy hides assumptions rather than testing them. [R1](#ref-source-5a6026d6455bf7a0)

## Experimental criteria

State the temperature floor, field, time window and probe used to exclude magnetic order or freezing. Compare local magnetic probes with thermodynamics, transport and structural controls. A low-temperature heat-capacity term or a continuum of excitations can be informative, but each has alternative explanations and sample dependencies. Do not use one such feature as a complete topological-state diagnosis. [R2](#ref-source-9d15cd9236072f5c) [R4](#ref-source-5e0dcd80e8b64a1b)

The organic compound kappa-(BEDT-TTF)2Cu2(CN)3 is an important candidate platform. Shimizu and colleagues reported a lack of conventional order to low temperatures, while the pressure work of Kurosaki and colleagues mapped neighboring electronic phases. These are separate observations: the pressure-induced superconducting region does not show that the insulating state's proposed fractionalized excitations are the pairing mediator. [R2](#ref-source-9d15cd9236072f5c) [R3](#ref-source-0443485ac20d3a8c)

## Limits and disputes

The candidate interpretation has continuing spin-gap and valence-bond-order questions, explicitly represented by the later source cited here. Pressure changes bandwidth and may change several effective parameters. A neighboring superconductor is therefore evidence of phase-diagram proximity, not a controlled demonstration of a QSL-to-superconductor microscopic route. [R3](#ref-source-0443485ac20d3a8c) [R4](#ref-source-5e0dcd80e8b64a1b)

Frustration is a useful route to suppressing simple order, but it is neither sufficient nor a universal necessary condition for every spin-liquid construction. Different topological labels and proposed excitations must be tied to a specified theoretical state. The pilot removes categorical material assignments and universal heat-capacity exponents from the original overview. [R1](#ref-source-5a6026d6455bf7a0) [R4](#ref-source-5e0dcd80e8b64a1b)

## Discovery implications

A testable proposal should predict a controlled change in fractionalization-sensitive and charge-coherence observables across a transition. Preserve negative evidence and disorder diagnostics. The strongest research lead is a mechanism-specific discriminating measurement, not the co-occurrence of superconductivity and a suggestive insulating phase diagram.
