## Scope

Cuprates share CuO2 planes as a central electronic motif, but they do not share one complete phase diagram. Layer count, apical oxygen, charge-reservoir blocks, disorder and the sign and amount of doping vary across families. The La-Ba-Cu-O discovery and the subsequent Y-Ba-Cu-O work established the high-temperature cuprate research platform; they do not make every later composition equivalent. [R1](#ref-source-ad4ee05105f93caf) [R2](#ref-source-9350a30b2eabe27e)

Many parent compounds are antiferromagnetic correlated insulators. Doping can produce superconductivity together with pseudogap behavior, charge correlations and unusual normal-state transport. A schematic dome is an organizing picture rather than a universal quantitative equation for all compounds. A weak-coupling phonon-only explanation is not a complete account of the family, while Cooper pairing and broken-symmetry descriptions remain useful. [R6](#ref-source-6c1991333d4a6fed)

## Mathematical assumptions

A frequently used illustrative form of a two-dimensional d-wave gap is

$
\Delta(\mathbf k)\propto\cos k_x-\cos k_y.
$

This lattice expression represents a symmetry structure in an appropriate square-lattice description. It is not a fit to every Fermi surface, and it does not specify a pairing mediator or an absolute gap magnitude. The significance of a sign change must be connected to phase-sensitive and momentum-resolved evidence in the named material. [R5](#ref-source-2e4d7fdb6c1797b7) [R6](#ref-source-6c1991333d4a6fed)

## Experimental criteria

Keep a transport onset, zero-resistance point, magnetic response and thermodynamic anomaly in separate fields. Record pressure, medium, oxygen content, preparation route and sample form. For a gap-symmetry claim, combine phase-sensitive tests with complementary spectroscopy and low-energy response. Tricrystal experiments provide an important phase-sensitive cuprate constraint; their interpretation should not be extended automatically to every electron-doped, highly disordered or pressure-treated sample. [R5](#ref-source-2e4d7fdb6c1797b7)

The familiar Hg-1223 examples illustrate the danger of unconditioned comparisons: the historical ambient-pressure transition near 133 K and a high-pressure scale near 164 K around 31 GPa concern different conditions. The pilot leaves an individual onset-versus-zero criterion unknown where this scoped review has not re-extracted it. That missing field must not be silently supplied by a search or an agent. ## Limits and disputes

Antiferromagnetic correlations, a pseudogap and charge order constrain a microscopic theory without selecting one unique pairing glue. A charge-density modulation is not by itself evidence of a pair-density wave. Likewise, a linear-temperature resistivity is not an automatic measurement of a Planckian scattering time. The observable and the inference must remain separate. [R6](#ref-source-6c1991333d4a6fed)

The source chapter includes a 2026 pressure-quench report and distinguishes an onset from zero resistance. Its new quantitative claims have not received a primary-text check in this pilot and are not included as verified comparison records. Metastable pressure-treated material must remain distinct from an equilibrium ambient-pressure phase. The omission and review limitation are preserved in the editorial mapping.

## Discovery implications

A useful mechanism predicts more than a plausible energy scale: it should connect an interaction model to gap symmetry, a material trend and a falsifiable observation. Spin-fluctuation, RVB and lattice-related approaches have different assumptions and outstanding tests. Treat their coexistence in a review as competing explanations, not a combined proof.
