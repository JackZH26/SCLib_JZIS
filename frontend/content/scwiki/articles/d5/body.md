## Scope

“Nickelate superconductivity” includes distinct structural and preparation routes. Infinite-layer RNiO2 films and bilayer Ruddlesden-Popper La3Ni2O7 crystals differ in layer geometry, oxygen coordination and electronic structure. Their temperatures should not be presented as continuous optimization of one material. Li and colleagues' 2019 discovery and Sun and colleagues' 2023 high-pressure report establish different experimental settings. [R1](#ref-source-35fd62b7cb3c490c) [R2](#ref-source-df0c15d62982f853)

The infinite-layer route uses epitaxial precursors and topotactic reduction. Oxygen stoichiometry, residual apical oxygen, reduction uniformity and the substrate can all matter. A nominal chemical formula does not prove identical electronic states across bulk powders, supported films and membranes. This pilot does not infer a universal exchange energy or a common one-band model from the resemblance of NiO2 and CuO2 planes. [R1](#ref-source-35fd62b7cb3c490c)

## Mathematical assumptions

An orbital label such as $d_{x^2-y^2}$ or $d_{z^2}$ identifies part of a model basis; it does not by itself identify the pairing channel. A proposed gap or coupling matrix needs a declared band structure, filling, interaction and structural phase. Similar local configurations can motivate a cuprate analogy, but do not transfer a phase diagram or a numerical pairing scale to nickelates. [R2](#ref-source-df0c15d62982f853)

Pressure changes can involve structural rearrangement as well as electronic bandwidth. For a bilayer crystal, a model evaluated in one structural phase must be compared with measurements in the same pressure regime. Distinguish calculations suggesting a favorable orbital configuration from observations establishing superconducting signatures. [R2](#ref-source-df0c15d62982f853)

## Experimental criteria

Record family, layer count, rare-earth composition, sample form, reduction procedure, oxygen content and substrate. Keep resistance onset, zero resistance, magnetic response and superconducting fraction separate. If a field-induced or re-entrant state is proposed, include field direction and the temperature of the measurement rather than substituting a characteristic field for an upper critical field.

The Li source reports a transition range around 9-15 K in infinite-layer films. This pilot does not assign precise onset and zero values without re-extracting those individual criteria. The Sun abstract reports a maximum scale of 80 K in La3Ni2O7 over pressures between 14.0 and 43.5 GPa with resistance and susceptibility evidence. The maximum is not asserted to occur at every pressure in that interval. [R1](#ref-source-35fd62b7cb3c490c) [R2](#ref-source-df0c15d62982f853)

## Limits and disputes

An 80 K high-pressure bilayer observation is not ambient-pressure bulk superconductivity. Likewise, a strained film and a pressure-treated crystal need not follow an identical structural path. The 2026 film, membrane, oxygen-cycling and magnetic-substitution updates in the pinned chapter require separate primary-text checks. This pilot retains their review debt rather than adding their numbers to a quantitative record. [R2](#ref-source-df0c15d62982f853) [R3](#ref-source-4b5b65a5047eb1a9)

The pairing symmetry and microscopic interaction remain inference questions. A theoretical s± candidate, an orbital-selective interpretation or a magnetic compensation scenario must keep its theoretical or hypothetical label until supported by the corresponding phase-sensitive or field-resolved evidence. [R2](#ref-source-df0c15d62982f853)

## Discovery implications

Compare controlled sample series within a structural family before making cross-family claims. A useful design experiment separates oxygen, strain, interface and carrier changes as far as possible. Negative controls are especially valuable when two substrates both produce compression but only one preparation yields a transition. Such observations constrain a mechanism without proving that one structural variable is sufficient. [R1](#ref-source-35fd62b7cb3c490c) [R3](#ref-source-4b5b65a5047eb1a9)
