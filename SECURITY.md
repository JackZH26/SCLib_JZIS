# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately to `info@jzis.org`. Do not
open a public issue with exploit details, credentials, personal data, or a live
proof of concept.

Include the affected URL or component, expected impact, reproduction steps, and
any safe mitigation you have identified. JZIS aims to acknowledge reports within
two business days, provide an initial assessment within five business days, and
coordinate disclosure after a fix is available. Good-faith testing must avoid
service disruption, data modification, account access beyond accounts you own,
and scanning infrastructure outside SCLib.

The `main` branch and the current production deployment are supported. Generated
data snapshots and archived research experiments are out of scope unless they
create a vulnerability in the supported service.
