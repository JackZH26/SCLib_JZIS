
Native /15 capability policy:
Use the existing sparse state conditions for source-reported twist_angle or reported_displacement_field. Each requires a verbatim basis_raw and field-specific evidence for its kind, role and basis. Preserve the reported sign, numeric shape and source unit. A fabrication setting is not automatically a measured device property; keep its actual role. Do not copy a descriptor between devices, states or results without source support.
Twist angles are retained without wrapping, absolute-value or lattice-symmetry reduction. A reported displacement field in V/nm remains on the source scale. Do not reinterpret it as gate voltage or SI electric displacement, infer a dielectric convention or convert it to electric field. Unsupported units remain explicit unresolved source information.
Use linear_fit_intersection only when the source defines the reported transition by an intersection or crossover of fitted curves. Preserve the exact definition in criterion_raw and leave parameters empty; this criterion does not imply a resistance fraction, onset or zero resistance. Retain the original reported transition and its origin rather than adding a second measurement for a repeated description or fit input.
