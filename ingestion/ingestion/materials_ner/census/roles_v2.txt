Choose the role of this occurrence's literal, rather than its surrounding topic:
- property_result: a quantitative material property, transition, characteristic
  scale, fitted material parameter or relative property comparison. Bounds,
  percentages, intervals and approximate values are quantitative. A physical
  property remains a result when used to describe a comparison sample.
- sample_descriptor: composition or sample geometry/dimensions. Do not use this
  role merely because a sentence describes a sample: transition temperatures,
  magnetic moments and other physical properties are property_result.
- measurement_condition: an imposed measurement setting, environment or sweep.
- preparation_condition: synthesis, processing or treatment settings.
- extrapolation_target: the variable value at which an extrapolated property is
  evaluated, separate from that extrapolated property value.
- behavior_window: the domain where a trend, fit or behavior applies.
- model_parameter: an adopted input, general conversion factor or relationship
  coefficient. A fitted or derived material property is property_result.
- qualitative_statement: a vague or qualitative assertion with no definite
  numerical expression. An explicit numeric inequality is not qualitative.
- symbolic_relationship: a functional dependence or equation as a whole.
- non_detection: an explicit absence of a specified phenomenon in a stated
  observation window. Lack of discussion is not absence.
- unresolved: insufficient or contradictory support for a role.

Origin is ownership of this particular claim, not the containing document.
Use cited_work for prior reported results or adopted knowledge attributed to
literature. Read the whole claim through its citation suffix; a clipped occurrence
quote may omit it. Use current_work when the paper's own observations, figures,
calculations or derivations support the claim. A cited method does not turn a
current result into prior work. A citation supporting a theoretical expectation
may share a sentence with a current observation. If attribution cannot be
resolved, use unspecified; do not default to current_work.

For property_result, sample_descriptor, model_parameter and non_detection, give
basis as measured, fitted, extrapolated, computed, derived, assumed or unspecified.
Use the relevant methods and captions, not just the local phrase. An explicitly
measured composition is measured. A number calculated from measurements or an
assumption is derived; assumed describes an adopted input, not a derived output.
A limiting value outside observed data is extrapolated. If an arithmetic
conversion is applied to an extrapolated parent, identify that relationship in
the note; its dependency must be retained in later graph construction. Use
unspecified when the source does not establish the basis. For all other roles,
basis must be null because this field is not applicable to that role.

Give a brief English note justifying role, origin and applicable basis. Do not
claim scientific approval. Source-unit support is necessary, not proof that
