# Host physical references

`/discovery/host-references` adds a source-qualified physical comparison to
Discovery. It uses formation energy per atom and the OptB88vdW electronic band
gap from the same JARVIS provider record. A separate table view adds published
Voigt bulk/shear moduli and the original elastic tensor. It is separate from the frozen native
scientific matrix and from the COD coordinate workspace.

## Captured source and selection

Kamal Choudhary, *JARVIS-DFT 3D dataset (jdft_3d.json)*,
[Figshare version 11](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.6815699.v11), CC BY 4.0.
The captured member is `jdft_3d-9-24-2025.json`, obtained from
[file 64391379](https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/64391379).

- ZIP: 48,447,610 bytes; SHA-256
  `f9e0a3309f0000d5de1ec9e49c93963109ea45e63f451103f8f6595d2eabf7f5`.
- Member: 254,061,560 bytes; SHA-256
  `9dacb55ac8371c7c2bf0332933b55c98e9cca6201e92eb9d6fcd03aab0975907`.
- All 93,902 records were scanned. All 184 records matching the 22 formulas
  explicitly supplied in the methodology are retained, in original order.
  No lowest-energy-only, first-hit or superconductivity-based selection is made.
- The formulas cover oxides, nitrides, carbides, borides and complex oxides.
  Every selected row declares `func=OptB88vdW` and `typ=bulk`.

The finite download stores each original object as a string, with its zero-based
position in the full source array. Source strings and their numerical tokens are
unchanged. Individual record hashes, canonical-coordinate-object hashes, the full
source archive/member hashes and independent download checksums are provided.
The canonical atoms hash uses Python `json.dumps(sort_keys=True,
separators=(",", ":"))`; it is a byte identity, not a structure equivalence test.

The wrapper is valid JSON. One original object, JVASP-95531, contains non-standard
`NaN` tokens in six elastic-tensor entries. They remain inside the preserved
string. Edition 1.1 also retains tensor tokens as strings in the derived snapshot,
including six explicit `"NaN"` strings for source review. They never become browser
numeric NaNs, zeros or axis values. All energy/gap values are finite and unchanged.

## Units and scientific scope

The [provider unit table](https://github.com/usnistgov/alignn/blob/f2366daa3413d28a825b46e34d001b5549b05a40/README.md#2-on-jarvis-dft-2021-dataset-regression)
labels formation energy as eV/atom and the OPT band gap as eV. The
[JARVIS energetics implementation](https://github.com/usnistgov/jarvis/blob/3b0c9d0f0c15759135de857dbc93e010db01eb29/jarvis/analysis/thermodynamics/energetics.py#L47-L67)
also explicitly divides the elemental-reference energy difference by atom count.
The input values are published DFT metadata, not predictions generated by an
ALIGNN model in this change.

Formation energy relative to elements is a thermodynamic descriptor. Negative
formation energy alone does not establish stability against other compounds,
phonon stability, chemical survival, finite-temperature free energy or synthesis.
The band gap is an electronic descriptor, not a superconducting gap, pairing
strength, carrier density, coherence measure or Tc. Zero is a retained source
value, not a universal declaration of metallicity. Cross-composition formation
energies do not supply a universal ranking of superconducting promise.

The record supplies coordinates and a provider identity; common JID does not
independently verify the exact input/output chain for both quantities. Individual
temperature, pressure, convergence and condition applicability remain unchecked.
No COD phase or SCLib material/sample is automatically associated by formula.
No catalogue values, RPS, native scientific release, ML approval or stable-host
certification are added. No new DFT or experimental result is claimed.

## Source findings that affect presentation

The imported `ehull` field is **excluded**. Its unit labels are inconsistent across
provider documentation, and same-composition differences in the captured file
require normalization review. If two structures use the same per-atom energy and
hull reference, their difference in energy above hull equals their formation
energy difference. Examples in this edition:

| Formula and records | Formation-energy difference (eV/atom) | Difference between stored `ehull` values |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Al2O3, JVASP-10076 minus JVASP-32 | 0.01654 | 0.0033 |
| BN, JVASP-63690 minus JVASP-135776 | 0.00421 | 0.0021 |
| MgO, JVASP-116 minus JVASP-115229 | 0.02970 | 0.0149 |

These differences suggest an unresolved normalization/reference inconsistency;
they do not establish its cause. The original hull generation run and reference
closure are not available in this metadata view. Do not multiply values by atom
counts, silently replace them, call the dataset scientifically rejected or use
them for an acceptance threshold. The current JARVIS code is useful method
documentation, not proof that a stored 2025 value was produced by that revision.

Four records have inconsistent `nat` and coordinate inventories:

| JID | Formula | Source nat | Coordinate atoms |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| JVASP-86503 | LaAlO3 | 5 | 10 |
| JVASP-63690 | BN | 2 | 6 |
| JVASP-152573 | HfO2 | 6 | 12 |
| JVASP-190301 | NbN | 4 | 8 |

Different cell representations may explain these differences, but this view has
not established that relationship. All four retain their quantities in the table
and download, with a local review note; their markers are withheld. **180 records
are plotted, 184 are retained.** Passing the remaining metadata checks is not a
scientific review or convergence certificate.

## Interaction and verification

The main Discovery page and coordinate workspace link to this separate view.
Native selects filter by family/formula and elastic-data availability. A table
property selector switches between energy/band gap and elastic properties while
retaining the inspected JID and page. Availability filters count supplied
properties, not physically accepted states, and reset the page and inspected
record. Empty intersections show an explicit no-matches state with disabled page
buttons. Fixed, linear physical axes retain
all eligible filtered records across the 12-row table pages; zero values stay on
the axis, and overlapping records are neither merged nor jittered. Inspecting a
row exposes the source JID, original numbers, space group, both atom inventories
and hashes. Filter changes reset the page and old inspected record. Detailed
interpretation and download provenance are collapsed by default. Whole-page
overflow is avoided while the plot and table scroll within labelled keyboard
regions on narrow screens. All interface copy is English.

Inspect moves focus to the record panel; closing it restores the originating
button. Browser verification exposed an SVG title hydration mismatch. A regression
test reproduced it in this new plot and in both existing Discovery source plots.
All three now render title/description text as single strings, preserving source
values and chart semantics rather than recovering through a client-side redraw.

Recreate the metadata from the shipped source subset:

```sh
python scripts/build_discovery_host_reference.py --check
```

Verify the extraction directly against the complete downloaded source:

```sh
python scripts/build_discovery_host_reference.py --check --archive /absolute/path/jdft_3d-9-24-2025.json.zip
```

The archive path is local and explicitly supplied; the builder does not download,
execute provider code, write a scientific database or submit calculations. The
full ZIP, member, record sequence and final artifacts must match. Invalid values,
wrong method/dimensionality, formula-coordinate disagreement, reordered rows and
incomplete subsets are rejected. The finite UI loader accepts only this snapshot.

This advances D1 reference coverage and D5 physical-coordinate exploration. A
validated stability/pairing design space still needs traceable hull/phonon or
other relevant stability evidence, calculation/structure associations and
domain-appropriate pairing/coherence data. Formation energy is not substituted
for those requirements.

Original edition 1.0 local acceptance: 10 Python tests, 30 related frontend tests including hydration
of all three Discovery source plots, TypeScript checks and the 66-page production
build passed. Full-archive extraction reproduced the committed subset and view
metadata. Actual browser downloads matched the shipped 175,071-byte metadata and
611,794-byte original-record wrapper. At 1280, 390 and 320 px, document width
matched viewport width; at 320 px, selects were 44 px high and the 704 px table
scrolled internally with the keyboard. NbN's second page retained the final
source records and withheld the cell-review marker. The final production preview
had no warning/error console entries after the hydration correction. These are
local checks; upstream CI, scientific review and production release remain
separate requirements.


## Elastic reference edition 1.1 (5 October 2026)

The pinned provider unit table labels `Kv` and `Gv` in GPa. The
[provider elastic-method documentation](https://jarvis-materials-design.github.io/dbdocs/jarvisdft/#elastic-tensor)
defines them as the Voigt bulk and shear modulus from finite-difference elastic
calculations on conventional cells. The
[pinned tensor parser](https://github.com/usnistgov/jarvis/blob/3b0c9d0f0c15759135de857dbc93e010db01eb29/jarvis/io/vasp/outputs.py#L779-L900)
documents bulk tensor units as GPa and converts the VASP kBar quantities by
division by ten. Its separate 2D/vacuum conversion is not applied to this
bulk-only reference. These references establish field definitions, not the
specific code revision or complete run provenance of every 2025 record.

| Source content | Records |
| --- | ---: |
| Both published Voigt moduli | 126 |
| Both moduli absent (`na`) | 58 |
| Finite 6 × 6 tensor | 132 |
| Tensor containing source NaN | 1 |
| Tensor absent (`na`) | 51 |

All 126 modulus pairs overlap the finite-tensor set. Six other records have finite
tensors but no published moduli; the display does not calculate replacement
moduli. The source's two negative bulk moduli and three negative shear moduli
remain unchanged, with no automatic scientific rejection rule. Missing source
values are labelled “Not supplied”; this describes this captured edition rather
than every possible source or physical applicability.

The matrix preserves every original numeric token (including signed zero and
scientific notation) and the provider's original indices 1-6. Its mapping to
Cartesian shear components and the crystal-axis convention has not been verified
against individual runs. No matrix rotation, averaging, symmetrization,
eigensystem or stability criterion is applied. Finite tensor entries or positive
Voigt averages alone are not mechanical-stability certification. In particular,
the anomalous JVASP-95531 tensor is inspectable with a local source-review note;
its six NaNs are not repaired or hidden.
