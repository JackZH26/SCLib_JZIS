# Sample-qualified native paper contexts

Material pages can contain retained measurements while their catalogue selection is unresolved. Reopening a paper also exposes methods and interpretation limits that cannot safely be assigned as normalized material properties. This change adds a finite reading page for FeTeSe, κ-H8-Br and La4Ni3O9.99, and distinguishes an unresolved selection from an unavailable source.

The page contains three captured editions, 13 short source summaries and 29 hash-only text locators. Source metadata and downloads are closed by default. Numeric qualifiers and sample boundaries remain in the summaries: the Fe NMR discussion concerns another composition; the organic magnetic interpretation near 15 K differs from superconducting phase coherence near 11.9 K; the nickelate onset is 23 K at 79.2 GPa, while the source describes a resistance drop below 5 K at 32.8 GPa. Nickelate field curves, computed stoichiometric La4Ni3O10, model eigenvalue λ and predicted structure remain separate from the measured sample and electron–phonon coupling.

Captured editions are `0911.4758v1` (8 pages), `cond-mat/0612431v1` (5 pages), and `2309.09462v4` (10 pages). Complete PDF capture supports bounded methods and claim review; no supplementary files were fetched. The official nickelate arXiv record declares the APS DOI, but publisher and ingestion byte equivalence are not established. Native source sample/state/result associations remain unestablished.

The reader requires the exact finite snapshot; caller edits to authority, text, locators, source URLs or normalized values are rejected. Links from Fe and nickelate details require the exact material, selected result and paper identities together. The organic context has a finite material backlink only because no supported selected result exists. The six sampled classification fields remain `not_extracted`; all source-summary normalized values are null. This adds no canonical field updates, imports, formal reviews, scientific acceptance, ML approval or new calculations.
